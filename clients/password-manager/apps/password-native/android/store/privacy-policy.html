Privacy Policy — Yada Password

Effective date: September 4, 2026
App: Yada Password (Android package io.yadacoin.passwordrotation)
Contact: replace-with-your@email.example

Yada Password (“the App”) is a self-custody password manager and authentication provider for the YadaCoin password-rotation protocol. This policy describes what data the App handles and how it is used.

1. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following in the App’s private storage on your device:

  • Vault configuration you enter (for example username, node URL)
  • BIP39 seed phrase and master password (second factor) you provide
  • Derived site credentials and rotation state for registered sites
  • Caller pins used to recognize trusted requesting apps (package name and signing certificate fingerprints on Android)

Your seed phrase and master password are intended to remain on your device. Do not back them up to untrusted cloud services. Anyone with your seed and master password can control the vault.

2. Data sent over the network

The App communicates only with the node URL you configure (a YadaCoin node with the password-rotation features you choose to use). Depending on the action you approve, that may include identity and transaction payloads, password hashes, public key material, and rotation/sync requests.

The App does not require a Yada-operated cloud account. If you point the node URL at a third-party or public node, that operator processes the protocol data you send. Prefer HTTPS node URLs so traffic is encrypted in transit. HTTP (for example a local development node) is not encrypted.

When verifying some HTTPS site identities, the App may fetch public files such as Digital Asset Links (/.well-known/assetlinks.json) from the relevant host.

3. Deep links and other apps

Other apps may open the App via yadapass:// links to request site registration or sign-in/rotation. On Android, the App attempts to identify the calling app (package and signing certificates) before you approve. Approval is under your control. Callbacks are directed to the attested caller when possible.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs in the App as distributed for this listing
  • No sale of personal data
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs unless listed in a later update to this policy

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Do not use the App if you are under the applicable age.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Data already sent to a node you configured is retained according to that node operator’s practices and the public or private nature of the ledger or APIs involved.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s behavior changes. The effective date above will be revised accordingly. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
